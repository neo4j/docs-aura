== Support Eligibility

=== Included

* AuraDB Professional
* AuraDB Business Critical
* AuraDB Virtual Dedicated Cloud
* AuraDS Professional
* AuraDS Enterprise
* AuraDB Professional Trial (with a credit card attached to the organization)

=== Not included

* AuraDB Free (Community support only via link:https://community.neo4j.com/[Community] and link:https://discord.com/invite/neo4j[Discord])

== Set Up Support Access

Organization Owners and Admins provide access to the Support Portal from the Neo4j Aura Console.

. Navigate to *Organization Settings → Support*
. Complete the Support Profile (timezone, company name, address)
. Add Authorised Support Contacts

Support access is granted only after the profile is completed and authorised contacts are added.

== Authorised Support Contacts

Only Authorised Support Contacts can submit cases in the link:https://support.neo4j.com/s/[Support Portal].

Organization Owners and Admins can add or remove contacts at any time.

=== Contact Limits

==== Aura PAYG

* AuraDB Professional / DS Professional – 5 contacts
* AuraDB Business Critical – 10 contacts
* AuraDB Virtual Dedicated Cloud / DS Enterprise – 10 contacts

==== Subscription Support Packages

* Regional Support – 10 contacts
* Global Support – 20 contacts
* Signature Support – 30 contacts

If you require additional authorised contacts, contact your account team.

== Creating a Case

. Register on the Support Portal using the email address designated as an Authorised Support Contact
. Set up Multi-Factor Authentication (TOTP required)
. Submit a case by selecting the relevant instance tier or *Other support enquiry*
. Provide a clear subject and detailed description

Include as much relevant detail as possible, such as:

* Start time of the issue
* Affected feature or surface
* Relevant Cypher queries
* Error messages
* Steps to reproduce

Additional user registration information can be found link:https://support.neo4j.com/s/article/Accessing-the-Support-Portal-the-first-time[here].
